Weather intelligence is not an avalanche forecast.

Open-Meteo snow depth, snowfall, temperature and wind are modeled grid estimates, not mountain station measurements; their accuracy can vary sharply with elevation, terrain and wind transport. “Powder Alert” only means ≥15 cm of modeled new snow, not that skiing is safe. Consult the linked official forecast (where available), recent observations, access restrictions and field conditions. Some East Coast locations have no dedicated avalanche bulletin.
